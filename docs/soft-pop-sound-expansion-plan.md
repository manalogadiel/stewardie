# Stewardie: louder Soft Pop sounds

Status: implemented locally — October 1, 2026; physical-device listening and iOS compilation remain release gates. This document adds to the [current sound implementation](permissions-tour-moments-notifications-plan.md#10-owner-follow-up--october-1-implemented). See the [implementation and verification record](soft-pop-sound-expansion-verification.md) for measured waveforms, local checks and remaining gates. The implementation below follows the approved plan.

## Goal and current state

Make important feedback easier to hear and add a small, consistent family of playful clay pops and warm bell tones. Keep ordinary navigation quiet. Sounds supplement visible feedback and never determine whether an operation succeeds.

Before this implementation, the app bundled `notification.wav`, `success.wav` and `capture.wav`, generated by `tool/generate_soft_pop_sounds.dart`. Android action playback used SoundPool at **0.4 gain**; the synthesized samples also had low amplitude. iOS action playback used SystemSoundID without an adjustable playback gain. Device notification volume is controlled separately by the operating system. Perceived loudness has not yet been measured on physical devices.

## 1. Make the existing sounds louder first

- Measure each file's peak and RMS before changing it. Master the short cues toward a **−3 dBFS peak ceiling**, with similar perceived loudness across the set, soft attacks and short fades. Avoid clipping, sharp clicks and harsh high frequencies. Peak normalization alone is not proof of equal loudness.
- Start Android action playback at **0.8 gain**, up from 0.4, then listen on a device before increasing further. Tune sample mastering and playback gain together instead of maximizing both blindly.
- Use controllable iOS action playback, such as AVAudioPlayer with an ambient audio session, to provide comparable volume adjustment while honoring the silent switch and allowing other audio to continue. Verify camera/audio-session interactions.
- Preserve OS volume, silent mode, Do Not Disturb and notification-channel preferences. Never raise global device volume automatically.
- Keep notification sound tuning separate from action sound tuning. Existing Android channel settings are OS-owned: do not create another channel just to override a user's mute or custom sound. Verify the resource URI and behavior of existing installations as well as clean installs.

## 2. Expand the asset family

Use the existing deterministic generator for original, reproducible WAV files. Share the same warm timbre, but distinguish actions by rhythm and pitch. Keep mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM and synchronize Flutter assets, Android raw resources and iOS bundle resources.

| Asset | Character / target duration | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| `notification.wav` | Clear three-note bell, 0.6–0.8 seconds | One device notification, respecting quiet hours and recipient settings |
| `success.wav` | Brighter rising chime, 0.4–0.6 seconds | Confirmed task completion; retain current trigger |
| `capture.wav` | Rounded camera pop, 0.15–0.25 seconds | Successful camera capture; retain current trigger |
| `saved.wav` | Small paired clay taps, 0.2–0.35 seconds | Confirmed task/calendar save or profile update |
| `moment_shared.wav` | Warm rising bubble, 0.35–0.5 seconds | Photo successfully published to its space, not merely selected or queued |
| `space_ready.wav` | Welcoming bell pair, 0.4–0.6 seconds | Confirmed space creation or accepted join; approval-pending is not a successful join |
| `mood_checked_in.wav` | Gentle rounded plop, 0.15–0.3 seconds | Confirmed mood check-in |
| `location_start.wav` | Two rising tones, 0.25–0.4 seconds | Confirmed live-sharing start in the chosen space |
| `location_stop.wav` | Two settling tones, 0.2–0.35 seconds | User stops local sharing immediately; pending server confirmation stays visible |
| `reaction_pop.wav` | Tiny elastic pop, 0.08–0.15 seconds | Optional immediate reaction tap feedback; never implies server confirmation |
| `attention.wav` | Soft low double tap, 0.2–0.3 seconds | Optional failed-save feedback alongside the visible error; disabled by default |

Do not add sounds to every button, map movement, person filter, loading animation, automatic refresh, background synchronization or mood expiry. Do not announce automatic location expiry with an in-app sound while backgrounded. Use one notification tone for activity types initially; avoid a competing tone for every notification category.

## 3. One sound dispatcher and simple settings

- Extend `lib/core/sound_feedback.dart` with a typed cue registry, platform gain support and a single dispatcher. Preload short action cues; playback must remain nonblocking and failures must not affect operations.
- Deduplicate confirmed-operation sounds using their operation IDs. A replayed outbox action or repeated response must not chime twice. Play only for the initiating account's foreground action, not another member's synchronized change.
- Coalesce rapid reaction taps and use a short cooldown (start at 200 ms). Give confirmation/attention cues priority over reaction pops; avoid stacked playback.
- Add **App sounds** and a compact volume control with a user-triggered preview in account settings. Default app sounds to on with the louder tuned level; preserve an existing explicit opt-out. Keep reaction feedback optional and attention sounds off initially.
- Persist settings per account and clear active playback on sign-out. Separate these controls from device notification permission/channel settings; include a route to system notification settings rather than pretending the app can override them.
- When the OS displays a notification, do not layer another app cue over its sound. Background push remains handled by the OS, using existing discreet text and quiet-hour rules.

## 4. Implementation order

1. Measure/remaster the three existing cues and adjust Android gain; establish iOS playback parity.
2. Generate the additional assets, add native resource registrations and validate file consistency.
3. Add the dispatcher/settings and connect confirmed saves, uploads, membership and mood actions.
4. Connect location start/stop without delaying collection shutdown. Add optional reaction and attention cues last.
5. Record focused checks and device listening results in a verification note linked from this plan.

## Acceptance and verification

- Every waveform is finite, unclipped, within the chosen peak ceiling and starts/ends smoothly. Report measured peaks and RMS; compare loudness by listening as well.
- A confirmed action sounds once; failed, queued, replayed or externally synchronized actions cannot play a misleading success cue. Rapid reactions cannot create an audio pile-up.
- App-sound off and volume settings survive restart/account switch without leaking preferences between accounts. Muted device notifications remain muted.
- Check Android and iOS at low/medium/high system volume, with headphones, silent/DND, other audio playing and camera capture. Verify notification playback foreground/background and existing-channel behavior separately.
- Run focused Dart checks and native compilation for changed integration code. Physical-device listening and iOS/APNs checks remain release gates until actually performed.

No paid audio service, OneSignal activation, billing change or public publishing is required. Existing inbox, tasks, photos and sharing must continue to work even when sound playback is unavailable.
