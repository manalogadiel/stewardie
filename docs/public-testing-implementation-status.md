# Public-testing completion status

September 30, 2026. This records the implementation against [the plan](public-testing-completion-plan.md). It is not a claim that the app is approved for public distribution.

## Implemented locally

- A deleted space disappears from the active selector after the confirmed deletion. Owner-requested cleanup can drain immediately, while the scheduled worker remains the fallback. Invite replacement is one batch, with owner checks and expiry inside the Firestore rule limit.
- The tour points to live controls instead of fabricated miniature examples. Its first-use account flag remains; replays are manual. New clay art covers the three onboarding cards, Couple and Other space types, Today rest, and Moments selfie. Transparent derivatives of the Family, Friends, and Organization references are retained alongside the originals. Sparse clay motifs and waves appear behind onboarding and all three app tabs.
- Optional profile photos work from the onboarding camera/gallery or Account Settings. The image is cropped, reduced, EXIF-stripped, and stored in the account's Firestore profile; reads require shared-space membership. A pending onboarding upload can retry after verification. Initials and colors remain deterministic when no photo exists.
- Space has the account settings gear, compact name field, owner rename action, icon tiles, and a single sheet handle in the changed sheets. The MapTiler pilot key is the plain `flutter run` default, with a Dart-define override. The mood write path calculates expiry at space-local midnight on the authenticated server, with a local UI expiry timer and gray question placeholder.
- FCM replaced OneSignal for optional transport. Devices register per signed-in account, rotate token, and remove the registered token on sign-out. The scheduled worker sends generic FCM messages after checking preferences, membership, and quiet hours; invalid tokens are pruned. Push taps open the account inbox, whose task/space actions recheck access. Firestore inbox activity remains independent of push.
- The Plus paywall describes unlimited retained, authorized task history. Basic remains intended to cover today and the previous three calendar days. Store-provided localized prices are used when checkout is enabled.

## Open implementation and verification limits

- **RevenueCat prices:** The signed-in Stewardie dashboard has a default offering with monthly and yearly Test Store products attached to `stewardie_plus`, but their saved prices are still **US$9.99 / US$79.99**, not the approved **US$4.99 / US$39.99**. RevenueCat says saved Test Store pricing cannot be edited; a replacement product is required. The new-product form did not visibly save during this pass, so neither the offering nor the price was changed. Test Store keys are excluded from release builds. A real store configuration, nonfounder purchase/restore/expiry test, and live webhook/reconciliation check remain before enabling checkout.
- **Basic history boundary:** Firestore's existing UTC cutoff can omit the first hours of the fourth *space-local* day for positive time zones. The Flutter-side local filter prevents older results being displayed, but cannot retrieve records blocked by rules. Resolve this through a trusted, space-time-zone-aware history endpoint or a carefully redesigned authorization rule before claiming an exact four-calendar-day window.
- **Notifications:** Task, help, ownership, join/leave, routine, reminder, and rename activity use existing durable paths. The full event inventory in the plan remains broader than the implemented events: plan changes/arrival, Moments and reactions, mood activity, location session status, invite decisions, and private upload/cleanup errors still need individual audited events or explicit no-alert decisions. FCM foreground/background, permission denial, token rotation, account switch, quiet hours, and Android notification tap need real-device tests. iOS needs APNs setup. Verify the FCM HTTP v1 API and service-account permission in Firebase; a successful worker deployment does not prove delivery.
- **Media and UI:** Cross-device profile photo visibility, the new art at small/enlarged layouts, sheet insets, tour first-use behavior, map tiles, mood midnight/DST behavior, camera and Moments remain device checks. Firestore profile photos are a small-pilot storage approach; size/quotas should be monitored before a wide rollout.
- **Space operations:** Authenticated owner rename/deletion drain and invite create/redeem still need two-account verification. Cleanup must be checked for completed jobs and media deletion, not inferred from a successful enqueue. An account-level failed-cleanup status and operator retry surface are not yet present.
- **Public release:** Android signing/package identity, working external privacy/terms/deletion/support pages, operator moderation process, abuse/quotas review, policy declarations, and invited physical-device pilot remain gates. No Play developer account or real Play product configuration exists. This work did not enable billing, OneSignal Growth, or public publication.
