# Stewardie — Subscription Plan v1

Updated: September 22, 2026.
Status: finalized product-planning baseline for the pilot. Not a deployed billing system or a public offer. The two tiers and pilot limits are approved; price is a launch target and the 5 GB allowance requires a cost check before public paid launch.

This supersedes earlier Free/Plus/Pro and per-space subscription proposals.

## 1. Model and pricing

| Tier | Price | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Free | Individual account |
| Personal Plus | Target US$3.99/month or US$34.99/year | One individual account across its authorized spaces |

Annual target pricing saves about 27% versus twelve monthly payments. Regional prices, taxes/store presentation, and final launch approval remain open. The earlier US$29.99 annual and per-space prices are superseded.

One person subscribes once. Other members keep their own plans. Plus does not upgrade a space or its members and does not confer admin rights, guardianship, membership, or private-content access.

## 2. Approved pilot limits

| Capability | Basic | Personal Plus | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spaces created/owned | 3 | 20 | Per account |
| Total space memberships | 20 | 50 | Per account, including owned spaces |
| Members per space | 20 | 20 | Shared space total, including owner |
| Active tasks per space | 300 | 300 | Shared, not multiplied by members |
| Completed-task history | Today + previous 3 days | Full retained authorized history | Per viewer |
| Photo storage | 100 MB | 5 GB, cost-check required | Per uploader account, pooled across spaces |
| Photo uploads per day | 10 | 100 | Per uploader account |
| Photos per task or moment | 1 | Up to 5 | Attachment rule below |
| Basic recurring routines | 5 per space | 5 per space | Shared |
| Advanced routines managed | None | 50 | Per account across spaces |
| Temporary live location | 15/30/60 minutes | Same | Consent-based; staged feature |

Core tasks, handoffs, moods, reminders, invitations, and privacy/safety controls are included in both tiers. Plus adds full-history search/filtering and extra personalization. Albums remain a candidate enhancement, not a paid-launch dependency. Detailed advanced recurrence patterns must be specified before implementation; no additional entitlement tier is planned.

## 3. Counting and enforcement

These operational definitions make the approved limits implementable:

- Space caps apply to creation, ownership transfer, and joining. Owned spaces also count toward total memberships. Merely administering someone else's space does not count as ownership.
- Space-level limits never stack with the number of subscribers. All users share the same 20-member and 300-active-task caps.
- Active tasks are non-completed, non-cancelled task records, including requested, accepted, unclaimed, and overdue work. Completing an existing task remains possible at the cap. Reopening or generating a task requires capacity.
- Routine quotas count enabled routine definitions, not each generated task. Generated tasks count toward the active-task cap. At the cap, do not silently drop an occurrence: mark it blocked/pending and notify its manager.
- History uses the completion timestamp and the space's configured time zone: today plus the prior three calendar dates. For September 22, Basic shows September 19–22. All unfinished/overdue tasks remain visible regardless of age.
- Full history means retained, authorized records; it does not restore deleted content, include a departed space, or promise permanent retention.
- Successful new photo uploads count toward a UTC-calendar-day quota; retries of the same upload do not count twice. Show the next reset time. Deleting a photo does not reset the daily counter.
- Use decimal MB/GB. Count stored processed images plus thumbnails against uploader storage. Compress photos toward 500 KB, maximum 2 MB per processed image; no video at launch. Target size is not a quality guarantee.
- For a shared task/moment, a Basic uploader may attach only when there are no existing photos; a Plus uploader may add up to five total, subject to edit permission. This prevents multiple Basic uploaders from stacking the cap. Existing attachments remain visible to authorized Basic members.
- Store entitlement, quota counters, and upload attribution on the backend. Enforce membership and role separately. Concurrent uploads, invites, transfers, and task creation must not bypass caps.

Example: a Plus user owns 10 spaces, has one subscription, and has 5 GB total storage—not 50 GB. A Basic teammate can complete a task generated by that user's advanced routine and view its shared photos without acquiring Plus.

## 4. At a limit or after downgrade

- Show usage and the applicable limit before a blocked action. Preserve existing authorized content and unfinished work.
- Cancellation retains benefits until the paid period ends. After expiry, apply Basic history visibility without deleting older task records because the subscription ended.
- If an account owns more than 3 spaces or belongs to more than 20, keep existing memberships and spaces usable. Prevent additions that exceed the relevant Basic limit; do not remove people or delete spaces.
- If storage exceeds 100 MB, block new uploads until below the Basic allowance or upgraded. Preserve existing authorized photo viewing/downloads. No automatic deletion solely because of downgrade during the pilot.
- Existing tasks and photo attachments remain usable even when above the new attachment allowance.
- Proposed launch behavior for advanced routines: pause future generation when their manager loses Plus; preserve already generated tasks and notify the manager and affected space admins. Allow an eligible Plus member with appropriate permissions to take over explicitly. Finalize this behavior and notice timing before billing ships.
- Account/content deletion, membership removal, reporting, blocking, stopping location sharing, and personal-data access never require Plus. They continue to apply independently of retention and payment.

Before public launch, define over-quota retention/notice periods, uploader departure/account deletion behavior, and routine-manager departure handling. Do not promise indefinite free storage of over-quota content.

## 5. Billing and privacy requirements

- Bind purchases to the signed-in Stewardie account, not a space. Show the account being upgraded.
- Verify purchase status on the backend; support restore, renewal, expiry, refunds, and account switching.
- Prevent accidental duplicate subscriptions for the same account.
- A subscription follows its owner across devices/spaces, never transfers automatically with space ownership, and cannot restore revoked membership.
- Review current store billing requirements for initial markets before implementing checkout. No child purchase flow in the pilot.
- No ads or sale of mood/location data in this plan. Premium features must not enable mood surveillance, rankings, or longer/always-on location tracking.
- Place upgrade offers in account settings and relevant history/storage-limit states; do not interrupt check-ins, task completion, or stop-sharing actions.

## 6. Pilot cost and launch gates

The free tier is a user-facing plan, not a promise of zero hosting cost. No paid cloud service or purchase implementation is authorized by this document.

Before public paid launch:
1. Measure storage, thumbnail, transfer, database, mapping, and support costs for light and heavy multi-space accounts, including free recipients.
2. Validate that the target price supports the proposed 5 GB allowance and the free-user population after applicable fees/taxes. If not, obtain approval for revised limits/pricing before advertising.
3. Confirm personal pricing and regional presentation; no lifetime or unlimited-storage offer.
4. Set published content retention, downgrade notices, recurrence lifecycle, and account-deletion behavior.
5. Resolve child-account/profile design, launch countries, commercial name clearance, and operating budget.
6. Implement and test subscription lifecycle and quota enforcement before taking payments.

Test cases: mixed Basic/Plus members in one space; a Plus account in 10 spaces; exact daily/history boundaries; retries and concurrent uploads; full task/member caps; duplicate purchases; expiry/refunds; downgrade while over quota; departure from a space; and attempts to bypass roles or view private content.
