# Stewardie Soft Pop Design Spec

## The Frame
- **Canvas**: `#FAF9F6` warm paper ground.
- **Surfaces**: `#FFFEFB` clean elevated cards with subtle soft shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(32, 38, 51, 0.06)`.
- **Primary Accent**: `#244BFF` electric blue for punchy callouts, pills, and key CTA highlights.
- **Characters**: Sky-blue, butter-yellow, and soft-rose clay mascots. Diffuse lighting, matte clay feel, cheerful expressions.
- **Transitions**: Smooth easing (`power3.out`, `back.out(1.5)` for clay pops).
