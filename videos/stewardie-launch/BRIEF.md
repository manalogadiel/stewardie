## Intent

A 90-second product launch video for Stewardie, a private shared-life mobile app for families, housemates, dormmates, and crews. The video introduces the Soft Pop clay mascots (Butter, Sky, Rose), demonstrates effortless responsibility tracking with the Today view, showcases private photo sharing via Moments, and highlights emotional connection with daily mood check-ins. Warm, friendly, welcoming, and tactile.
